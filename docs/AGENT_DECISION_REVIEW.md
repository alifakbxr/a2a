Independent observable agent-decision review (#12). After running compare-agent-traces, reviewers may inspect the private transcript and annotate observable steps with a2a-independent-decision-review-v1. Run: node scripts/review-agent-decisions.mjs local-transcript.jsonl independent-review.json . Review JSON must contain reviewer.role=independent and annotations with step_index, area (planning, diagnosis, tool-selection, verification, error-recovery, handoff), grade (supported, incomplete, contradicted, not-observable), and an opaque evidence_ref such as trace:step10. The report only validates whether referenced steps exist, summarizes review judgments and avoids weighted reasoning scores; it cannot certify reviewer identity or verify raw evidence. Never expose private reasoning, prompts, logs or personal workspace paths. References: https://antigravity.google/docs/cli/headless/ and Google DeepMind research on code evaluation https://deepmind.google/blog/competitive-programming-with-alphacode/ .
