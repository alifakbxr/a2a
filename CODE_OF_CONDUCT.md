# Code of Conduct

PILAH aims to be a welcoming, respectful, and productive open-source community for people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully, including when disagreeing about technical decisions.
- Offer constructive, evidence-based feedback and accept good-faith corrections.
- Respect others' privacy, boundaries, time, and contributions.
- Make participation accessible and avoid assumptions about skill level or identity.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexual harassment, publishing private information without consent, impersonation, spam, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Retaliation against anyone reporting misconduct in good faith is prohibited.

## Scope and reporting

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other official project spaces. Report concerns privately through a contact method listed on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/alifakbxr), if available. For abuse on GitHub, use [GitHub's abuse reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). Do not publish sensitive incident details in issues. If no private contact is available, request one without sharing incident details.

Maintainers should handle reports discreetly, consider relevant context, avoid retaliation, and take proportionate action, including warnings, removal of content, locking conversations, or restricting participation. If the maintainer is involved or a conflict of interest exists, use GitHub's reporting channels directly.

This policy complements the [GitHub Community Guidelines](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines).
